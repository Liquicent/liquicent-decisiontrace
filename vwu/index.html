    Verified work units¶

    A verified work unit (VWU) is one completed, accepted and reversible unit of work that met its quality and governance thresholds. The definition comes from the WorkRank paper. It counts outcomes, not effort, time or process.

    A unit is recorded on an accepted outcome record, so the decision itself is never edited:

    dt outcome 01M4ZDRZG0 --accepted --vwu code.change \
  --verified-by automated --reversal-days 3 --reversal-cost 150 \
  --quality-metric ci.required_checks/v1 --quality-threshold 1 --quality-value 1
dt outcomes detect --git . --vwu      # merged, unreverted commits become code.change units
dt vwu                                # every unit, its state, and who did the work

    What a unit records¶

    Field Holds
    unit What one unit is, a dotted label: code.change, support.ticket, sales.close
    quantity Units delivered (absent means 1)
    executor Who did the work (absent means the decision's actor)
    quality The predefined threshold: metric id, threshold, value, baseline, direction
    measured The measurement window: started_at, ended_at
    verification method (human, automated, outcome_signal, precedent, sample), at, verifier, ref
    reversal window_ends_at (until when it can be undone) and max_cost_usd (what undoing costs)

    A value that misses its threshold is not a unit and is rejected. The same goes for a window that ends before the verification, and for a unit on any status but accepted.

    Who earns from a unit¶

    Whoever executed it: a person, an agent, or a vendor's person or agent. The Actor record carries org, the accountable organisation the executor works for, and version, an agent's model, prompt and tool bundle. Trust earned by one agent version need not carry to the next. DecisionTrace records who did the work; it never scores them. Trust, routing and settlement need many people's records and live in Liquicent.

    State¶

    A unit's state is derived when you read it, never stored:

    • provisional: still inside its reversal window.
    • final: the window has ended with no cancel. A cancel after the window does not reopen it.
    • reverted: a later outcome for the same decision was reverted, failed or overridden inside the window. A complaint or an escalation does not undo delivery.

    The number to watch¶

    dt vwu ends with the share of units verified without a person. When agents produce far more units than people can review, that share decides how fast work can be accepted. Checks that run by themselves (automated, outcome_signal, precedent, sample) are what keep review from becoming the bottleneck.
